# Getting Support for Chorus

We want your experience with Chorus to be seamless. Here are the best ways to get help, ask questions, or report issues:

---

## 📖 Technical Documentation

Before opening an issue, check the official documentation portals:
- **Documentation Portal:** [docs.joinchorus.app](https://docs.joinchorus.app)
- **API Reference:** the REST API section of [docs.joinchorus.app](https://docs.joinchorus.app)
- **Architecture RFCs:** [joinchorus/docs/rfcs](https://github.com/joinchorus/docs/tree/main/rfcs)

---

## 💬 Community Discussions & Questions

For general questions, design ideas, architecture RFCs, and discussions:
- Join the conversations in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/joinchorus/chorus/discussions).

---

## 🐛 Bug Reports & Feature Requests

- **Bug Reports**: If you find a bug in Chorus, search existing issues first. If not found, open a [Bug Report](https://github.com/joinchorus/chorus/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Feature Proposals**: Open a [Feature Request](https://github.com/joinchorus/chorus/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) to discuss potential additions.

---

## 🔒 Security Inquiries

For sensitive security disclosures, please follow our [Security Policy](https://github.com/joinchorus/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) or email `security@joinchorus.app`.

---

## ✉️ Direct Contact

For administrative inquiries:
- Email: `support@joinchorus.app`
